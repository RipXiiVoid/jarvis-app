Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Phone controls, the wake word and listening run on your phone for free. For chatting, one tap connects a free OpenRouter account that gives your phone its own free AI, about 50 answers a day. Without that, a free guest brain still answers one question every 30 seconds, and you can download an offline brain too.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, as a web app. Open this page on the iPhone, tap Get for iOS, then Share › Add to Home Screen. Apple doesn’t let other assistants listen in the background or control the phone, so on iPhone you tap the orb to talk. Tip: make a Shortcut that opens it, and “Hey Siri, Whitehole” launches it.

Why does it show a “Listening…” notification?

Android only lets an app use the microphone in the background if it shows a notification. Whitehole keeps it silent and as small as Android allows.

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

Only install apps from people you trust. This page and the download come straight from the developer’s GitHub. Whitehole asks for the microphone, contacts (to call people by name) and “appear on top” (to pop up over other apps).

It doesn’t hear me in the background on my Samsung

Open Settings › Apps › Whitehole › Battery and choose Unrestricted. Then check Whitehole isn’t under “Sleeping apps” in Settings › Battery › Background usage limits.
